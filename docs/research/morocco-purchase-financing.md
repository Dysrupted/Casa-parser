# Buying an apartment in Casablanca: costs, credit and MRE rules

Research date: **2026-11-12**. Buyer profile: one buyer, probably a Moroccan living abroad (MRE)
paid in EUR, 80–100 m² resale flat, 1–3.5 M MAD, Aïn Diab / Anfa / Racine / Gauthier.

## How to read this

- **Method limits.** This was desk research through web search only. The session could not open
  pages directly (bkam.ma, ancfcc.gov.ma, oc.gov.ma, tax.gov.ma and the press sites were all
  outside the network allowlist), so every figure comes from search-result extracts of the pages
  cited. No official text was read in full. Treat "high" as "several independent sources agree
  and the legal reference is known", not as "verified against the Bulletin officiel".
- **Source quality.** Official figures (Bank Al-Maghrib, the 2016 land-registry decree, the 2026
  exchange-control instruction) are reported via the press. Most fee and bank-condition figures
  come from commercial guides (reaconsult.ma, lesmre.com, wafir.ma, baytima.com) that contradict
  each other; wafir.ma in particular gives inconsistent numbers between its own pages (policy rate
  2.25 % vs 2.75 %) and is used only as corroboration.
- **Confidence**: high / medium / low, per figure. **Date** = publication or entry into force when
  known, otherwise "2026" (page presented as a 2026 guide) or the consultation date.
- Nothing here is a bank quote. The config values are defaults to replace with the bank's written
  offer and the notary's fee note.

## 3. MRE specifics

- **Dirham loan from a Moroccan bank** is the standard route. Attijariwafa (via Attijariwafa bank
  Europe), Banque Populaire (Chaabi Bank), CIH and BMCE/Bank of Africa run MRE desks and take
  files from abroad ([lesmre](https://www.lesmre.com/fr/guides/credit-immobilier-mre-maroc), 2026, low).
- **Exchange-control rule (Office des Changes, Instruction générale 2026, art. 793, in force
  2026-01-01):** banks may lend dirhams to MRE for buying or building property up to **80 % of the
  price** (70 % before), for any number of properties. The rest — down payment and the purchase
  costs (notary, duties) — must come from foreign currency: a transfer from abroad or a debit of a
  foreign-currency / convertible-dirham account. Repayments (capital, interest, fees) must also be
  made by selling foreign currency or from a convertible-dirham account
  ([oc.gov.ma](https://www.oc.gov.ma/fr/mre/credits-pour-lacquisition-ou-la-construction-de-biens-immeubles),
  [bladi](https://www.bladi.net/immobilier-maroc-mre-financer-jusqu-credit,122290.html),
  [snrtnews](https://snrtnews.com/fr/article/office-des-changes-ce-que-change-la-reglementation-des-changes-2026-pour-residents-mre-et), medium).
- **20 % or 30 % down?** Older Office des Changes material and several 2026 guides still state a
  minimum **30 %** foreign-currency contribution. The 80 % ceiling implies 20 %. Individual banks
  may keep 30 %. Unresolved: ask the bank (hence the "cautious" scenario).
- **Repatriation on resale.** Proceeds can be transferred abroad to the extent the purchase was
  funded in foreign currency (currency sold, plus loan principal repaid in currency), plus the
  capital gain, on presentation of the sale deed and tax clearances (art. 795 / 171 as reported).
  Keep every "formule de cession de devises", transfer order and convertible-account statement;
  a purchase paid from an ordinary dirham account can block the transfer
  ([bladi](https://www.bladi.net/mre-erreur-moment-acheter-maroc-bloquer-rapatriement-argent,123833.html),
  [reaconsult](https://reaconsult.ma/tendances-et-conseils-en-immobilier/rapatrier-fonds-vente-immobiliere-maroc-office-changes-mre), 2026, medium).
- **Proof of income** usually asked: last three payslips, employment contract, latest foreign tax
  return, bank statements; some banks want sworn translations
  ([wafir](https://wafir.ma/fr/guide/mre-france-credit-immobilier-maroc-2026-guide-complet), 2026, low).
  Whether banks discount foreign income when computing the debt ratio was not found.
- **Borrowing in Europe instead.** No reliable source on French/EU banks taking a mortgage on a
  Moroccan property; what is reported is a personal loan in France (short, ≤ 5–7 years) used as
  part of the down payment, or a loan secured on property owned in Europe
  ([expat.com forum](https://www.expat.com/fr/forum/afrique/maroc/708570-faire-credit-en-france-pour-achat-au-maroc.html), low).
  A EUR loan removes currency risk on repayments; a foreign personal loan counts in the debt ratio.
- **Currency risk.** Income in EUR, loan in MAD. The dirham is managed against a EUR/USD basket
  (60 % / 40 %, ±5 % band — background knowledge, not re-checked in this research), so EUR/MAD
  moves little; the simulation already shows a ±5 % band on EUR amounts.

## 5. Public aid

- **Aide directe au logement ("Daam Sakane", 2024–2028):** 100 000 MAD for a home ≤ 300 000 MAD,
  70 000 MAD for 300 000–700 000 MAD (tax included); Moroccans in Morocco or abroad, first-time
  buyers, main residence for 5 years, new housing
  ([medias24](https://medias24.com/2023/10/17/le-roi-mohammed-vi-preside-une-reunion-sur-lhabitat-les-aides-au-logement-fixees/),
  [bladi](https://www.bladi.net/aides-logement-maroc-conditions-modalites-octroi,104774.html), 2023–2026, high).
  **Not applicable here: the budget (1–3.5 M MAD) is above the 700 000 MAD ceiling.**
- **Damane Assakane (FOGARIM / FOGALOGE guarantees, Tamwilcom):** state guarantee for low or
  irregular incomes and middle-class / MRE buyers; published ceilings are inconsistent (price
  250 000 MAD for FOGARIM; loans up to 800 000 MAD for FOGALOGE in an older article). **Not
  relevant for a 1.3 M+ MAD loan** ([fnh](https://fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/damane-assakane-2024), low).

## 7. To confirm with a bank and a notary

1. The rate offered (fixed, before or after the 10 % VAT), the duration and the APR.
2. Minimum down payment for an MRE at that bank: 20 % or 30 %, and that it must arrive in foreign currency.
3. Insurance: rate, base (initial or outstanding capital, or single premium), and the age loading.
4. Dossier and valuation fees; early-repayment penalty; mortgage release cost.
5. Debt-ratio ceiling for foreign income and which documents (translations) are required.
6. Notary: fee note (rate, VAT, stamps), the mortgage-registration scale, and how the payment must
   be routed to avoid the +2-point surcharge and to keep the right to repatriate.
7. For an agency listing: commission rate, who pays, VAT included or not.
